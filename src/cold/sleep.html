--- date: 2026-07-08 filename: sleep.html preview: My personal mental trick that always cures my acute insomnia. type: post --- # How To Fall Asleep _I'm not a doctor. This is not medical advice. I _usually_ have no trouble falling asleep. But sometimes I do. This is how I fix it._ ## Give Yourself Three Chances Stop tossing and turning. Whatever position you're in when you are reminded of this method, that's the position you must stay in. Now, allocate yourself three "toss and turn" charges. You can use a charge to scratch an itch, or reposition yourself, or get up and use the bathroom or whatever. Whatever you need to do. But you must commit to yourself that once you are out of charges, you must stay still for the rest of the night no matter what. It's okay to do multiple things using one charge, as long as you do them all at once, or in immediate sequence. No pausing betweenn actions. Each time you pause and want to move again, you must use another charge. What will happen is you will start to plan how you use your charges tactically. Maybe you'll defer scratching an itch because you expect another one will come up, and you can knock them both out with one charge. This is good. I've never gotten to zero charges. I can be tossing and turning for hours, then decide to do this plan, and once I do I'm always asleep within 20 minutes. ## Why Does It Work? Hell, maybe it doesn't work. Maybe it's placebo. What the hell do I know. But it works for me so I do it. My best guess is that trouble falling asleep leads to tossing and turning, but tossing and turning itself prevents you from falling asleep. Forcing yourself to stop calms your body down. (Maybe. I really don't know and this is all speculation.)
