# Harnesses Are Just Brain Tricks

The chronology of harness engineering shares a lot with that of human development.
Here are some problems that both humans and agents experience,
and their respective solutions:

It's hard not to say the human and agent solutions look alike.

Some of these are extreme, but so were the ones in the first table.
In fact in the time since I started thinking about this topic,
several rows moved from the second table to the first.

I have to admit that even though it makes sense that this pattern would hold,
I still find myself dumbfounded in the ways that it does.
They somehow seem obvious in retrospect,
but surprising in how minimal the right solution actually turned out to be&mdash;both for human development and harness engineering.
